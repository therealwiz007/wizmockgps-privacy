Wiz Mock GPS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

Wiz Mock GPS is a GPS location simulation and testing utility. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information necessary for the app to function.

Information We Collect

Wiz Mock GPS does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

Locations searched for or selected within the app are used to provide the requested map and location functionality. The app does not operate a server that stores your selected locations or personal information.

Internet and Third-Party Services

The app may connect to third-party services to provide map, location search, geocoding, or related functionality. These services may receive technical information required to process your request, such as the location or search query you submit.

Wiz Mock GPS does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Saved Locations

Locations saved within the app are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to a Wiz Mock GPS server.

Advertising

Wiz Mock GPS is ad-free. The app does not contain advertising SDKs or display advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Wiz Mock GPS is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the app and its associated functionality. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
